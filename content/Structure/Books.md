# Highlight Key
---
Before you go deeper, please refer to this key. It will unlock the intention behind my highlights, and perhaps you too will take interest in the collections I have formed.

---

> [!quote] Gray = Quotes

> [!info] Blue = Concept

>[!check] Green = Insight

> [!question] Yellow = Question / Disagreement

**First Read - Inspectional Reading (15 minutes)**
- What are the main ideas?
- What is the author trying to accomplish?
- What is the structure of the chapters?
- What is the context?

**Second Read - Analytical Reading**
- Read the book slowly and deeply
- Apply the color coded highlight system
- When the context is understood through inspectional reading the impulse to highlight everything lessens
- Export the highlights and try to expand upon the blue and green highlights
