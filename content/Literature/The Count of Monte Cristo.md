# The Count of Monte Cristo

**Alexandre Dumas**
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

![Title|300](https://external-content.duckduckgo.com/iu/?u=https%3A%2F%2Fimages-na.ssl-images-amazon.com%2Fimages%2FS%2Fcompressed.photo.goodreads.com%2Fbooks%2F1724863997i%2F7126.jpg\&f=1\&nofb=1\&ipt=60d8ccd838d46f4c876506c9ccb15ffcb2f1d126e4ddc91acdf69c308fc3137f)

> [!summary]
> This book is a classic for a reason. Dumas follows the journey of Edmond Dantes. Edmond is a virtuous young man who quickly earns the respect of the reader. He is set to earn a promotion on his ship, marry the woman he loves, and enjoy his life thoroughly. However, this is all interrupted when three of his acquaintances set him up for arrest to satisfy their greed, jealousy, and negligence. Edmond loses everything and is wrongfully imprisoned for fourteen years before spending the rest of his live in service to providence, bringing down from the heavens above an elaborately planned vengeance upon those who wronged him, and a divinely ordained justice to those who didn't. This book explores the themes of resilience, power, love, and faith.
