> [!summary]
> This article discusses how generative AI integration should be approached from an organizational behavioral management lens. The author suggests that the behavioral repertoires gained through the use of generative AI may act a cusp behaviors rather than simply expanding the efficiency or scope of existing behaviors. This has far reaching implications for an organization. As much as it capable of reshaping an organization in a productive way, it could also increase systematic bias, paralyzing surveillance, or human reliance upon generative tools without any critical analysis. Due to these potential consequences, AI integration should not simply be treated as the integration of a new technology or tool, but as technology that could enable behavioral cusps capable of reshaping the environment, reinforcers, and stimulus controls.
