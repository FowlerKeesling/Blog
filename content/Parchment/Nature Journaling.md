# Note
---
Nature journaling is a practice that encourages a deeper connection to the life around you. It is a technique that involves only three questions.

> 1. What do you notice?
> 2. What questions do you have?
> 3. What does it remind you of?

Simply by asking yourself these questions you invite yourself into a deeper awareness of the world around you and strengthen your relationship to nature. However, by taking it a step further and using a journal to explore these questions through the art of writing or sketching, you can ground your thoughts physically and offer the world a gift: your undivided attention.
