# Ingredients
---
- [ ] 1 lb ground beef or turkey
- [ ] 1 green bell pepper
- [ ] 1 medium yellow onion
- [ ] 4 cloves garlic
- [ ] 1 28 oz can diced tomatoes
- [ ] 1 6 oz can tomato paste
- [ ] 1 16 oz can tomato sauce
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- [ ] 2 tsp dried oregano
- [ ] 2 tsp dried basil
# Instructions
---
1.  In a large saucepan, cook the ground turkey, onion, garlic, and green pepper over medium-high heat.
2. Stir in diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, and tomato paste into the pan. Season with oregano, basil, salt, and pepper.
3. Simmer the sauce for 1 hour, stirring occasionally.
