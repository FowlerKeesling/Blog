# Ingredients
---
**Salad:**
- [ ] 2 Cups uncooked quinoa
- [ ] 2 English cucumbers
- [ ] 1 Red bell pepper 
- [ ] 1 Yellow bell pepper
- [ ] 1 Red onion
- [ ] 1 Box of cherry tomatoes
- [ ] 1 Cup fresh Italian parsley
- [ ] 3 Cans of chickpeas
- [ ] 1 Plain hummus
- [ ] Kalamata olives
- [ ] Feta cheese

**Dressing**
- [ ] 6 tbsp tahini
- [ ] 4 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] Juice of 2 lemons
- [ ] 2 garlic clove, minced
- [ ] 1-2 tbsp water (to thin)
- [ ] Salt and pepper, to taste
# Instructions
---
1) **Cook the Quinoa:** In a medium pot, bring 4 cups of water to a boil. Add the quinoa, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes or until the water is absorbed and the quinoa is tender. Set aside to cool.
2) **Chop the Veggies:** Chop the veggies into bite-size portions, halving the tomatoes and dicing the parsley.
3) **Mix Dressing:** Mix the dressing ingredients together in a bowl and transfer to a jar.
4) **Roast the Chickpeas:** Rinse and dry the chickpeas, then place them on a baking sheet. Drizzle some dressing over them and add some paprika. Place the chickpeas in the oven for 20 minutes at 350 F.
5) **Assemble Salad:** Layer quinoa, chickpeas, dressing, and veggies. Top it all with feta cheese.
