# Ingredients
---
**Noodles:**
- [ ] 8 oz [wide rice noodles](https://amzlink.to/az0boaMdihNPe)
- [ ] 2 Tbsp [sesame oil](https://walmrt.us/3S8Ajha) , (or canola)
- [ ] 2 shallots, chopped
- [ ] 2 carrots, thinly sliced
- [ ] 1 large chicken breast, chopped (or shrimp or tofu)
- [ ] 3 large cloves of garlic, minced
- [ ] ½ teaspoon fresh minced ginger
- [ ] 1 zucchini, thinly sliced
- [ ] 1 green bell pepper, thinly sliced
- [ ] 2 green onions, chopped
- [ ] 1 roma tomatoes, sliced
- [ ] 1 cup fresh Thai Holy Basil leaves (or substitute regular basil), , roughly chopped

**Sauce:**
- [ ] 3 tbsp oyster sauce
- [ ] 1/3 cup soy sauce
- [ ] 2 tsp fish sauce
- [ ] 2 tsp brown sugar
- [ ] 2 tbsp water
- [ ] 1 tsp thai red chili paste
# Instructions
---
1) **Cook noodles** according to package instructions.
2) **Mix sauce** in a small bowl and set aside.
3) **Cook Chicken:** Tenderize the chicken breasts, season generously with salt, pepper, and other seasonings, sear on both sides until fully cooked.
4) **Saute:** Using olive oil, saute all the veggies starting with the carrots and shallots, then when they are cooked, add in the rest of the ingredients.
 5) **Add** noodles to the pan and pour the sauce over the noodles. Toss and cook for a few minutes until warmed through. Remove from heat and stir in chopped basil.
