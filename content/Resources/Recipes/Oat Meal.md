# Ingredients
---
**Oats:**
- [ ] 1/2 cup rolled oats
- [ ] 1 cups soy milk
- [ ] 1/3 tsp cinnamon
- [ ] 1 banana
- [ ] 1 tbs ground flaxseed
- [ ] 1/4 tsp vanilla extract

**Toppings:**
- [ ] 1/4 cup stewed berries
- [ ] 1 tbsp peanutbutter
- [ ] 1/2 tbs hemp seeds
# Instructions
---
1. In a pot, combine the oats, milk, cinnamon, and half of a banana. Cook at a gentle simmer for five minutes stirring often.
2. Add the other banana and the flaxseeds, cooking for 3-5 minutes longer. Add more milk for a thinner oatmeal, or cook longer for a thicker oatmeal.
3. Remove the pot from the heat, stir in the vanilla, plate the oatmeal, and add the toppings.
