# Ingredients

---

- 2 lbs lean ground beef
- shredded cheese
- sour cream
- 1 onion
- 1 jalapeno
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 green bell pepper
- 14.5 oz can crushed tomatoes
- 14.5 oz can diced tomatoes
- 1 tbs tomato paste
- 19 oz can red kidney beans
- 1 1/2 cups chicken broth
- 1 cup beer
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 1/2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- salt and pepper to taste

# Instructions

---

1. Combine ground beef and chili powder.
2. In a large pot, cook the ground beef with the onion, jalapeño, and garlic. Drain any fat.
3. Add in the remaining ingredients and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer uncovered for 45-60 minutes.
4. Top with shredded cheese and sour cream.
