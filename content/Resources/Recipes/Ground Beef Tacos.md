# Ingredients
---
<font color="#00b0f0">Spanish Rice</font>
- [ ] 1 tbsp oil 
- [ ] 3/4 cup rice 
- [ ] 1/3 cup tomato sauce 
- [ ] 2 cups of chicken broth warmed 
- [ ] 1 tsp garlic powder 
- [ ] 1 tsp onion powder 
- [ ] 1 pinch of oregano 
- [ ] 1/2 tsp cumin 
- [ ] 1 tbsp bouillon chicken 
- [ ] 1 tsp salt 

<font color="#00b0f0">Tacos</font>
- [ ] 1 lb ground beef or turkey
- [ ] 1 packet of taco seasoning
- [ ] 1 can sweet corn
- [ ] 1 can black beans
- [ ] 3 avocados
- [ ] mexican blend shredded cheese
- [ ] sour cream
- [ ] hot sauce
- [ ] corn tortillas
# Instructions
---
<font color="#00b0f0">Spanish Rice</font>
1. Brown rice with oil in a heavy cast iron pan
2. Add sauce and spices and let them sit for 30 seconds
3. Add broth and cover with a lid. Don’t stir, and keep the lid on for at least 10 minutes.

<font color="#00b0f0">Tacos</font>ㅤ
1. Cook Spanish rice
2. Cook the turkey with seasoning
3. Warm tortillas on stove top
4. Add sour cream to tortillas
5. Add ground turkey and toppings
