# Ingredients

---

- 1lb package of uncooked gnocchi
- 4 tbsp of butter
- 2 large zucchini
- 4 cloves of garlic
- 1 lemon juiced
- 2/3 cup of grated Parmesan cheese
- 2/3 cup chopped basil leaves
- salt and pepper to taste

# Instructions

---

1. Boil a large pot of salted water
2. Melt one tbsp of butter in a large pan over medium-high heat. Sauté the zucchini for 4-6 minutes, then add the garlic and mix until fragrant.
3. Add the gnocchi to the boiling water and then strain the water when the gnocchi float to the top.
4. Add the gnocchi to the zucchini-garlic mixture and add the remaining tablespoon of butter, lemon juice, Parmesan, basil, salt and pepper.
5. Garnish with extra Parmesan and basis.

> [!note]
> If the mixture is dry, you may add back some of the starchy water to the pan.
